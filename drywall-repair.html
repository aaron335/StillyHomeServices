Common Questions

Is my repair too small?

Probably not. Small drywall repairs are exactly what I take on, and it’s easy to bundle several patches, touch-ups or small fixes into one visit.

Will I be able to see the patch?

The goal is that you can’t. Repairs are sanded, primed and painted to match the surrounding wall. Some older paint colors or sheens are hard to match exactly, so sometimes painting the full wall gives the cleanest result — I’ll tell you up front if that’s the case.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Send a few photos by text to (425) 238-2226 or fill out the form below, and I’ll follow up — usually within a day — to get you a quote or set up a quick walkthrough.

Are you licensed and insured?

Who actually does the work?
